How It Works

📊 Default Incentive Tiers

• Tier 1 (75–<85%): 1.5% of net revenue
• Tier 2 (85–<101%): 2.5% of net revenue
• Tier 3 (101–<111%): 3.0% of net revenue
• Tier 4 (111%+): 3.5% of net revenue
• Below 75%: No incentive

💰 Payout Structure

P1 - Equal Pool (Default 60%)
Divided equally among all staff members
P2 - Personal Pool (Default 40%)
Weighted by individual contribution to team sales

🔢 Example Calculation

Scenario:
• Team Target: AED 700,000
• Team Performance: AED 735,000 (105%)
• Tier: 3.0% rate
• Staff: 25 people
• Your contribution: 10%
Results:
1. Total Pool = 735,000 × 3% = AED 22,050
2. P1 Pool = 22,050 × 60% = AED 13,230
3. P2 Pool = 22,050 × 40% = AED 8,820
4. Your P1 = 13,230 ÷ 25 = AED 529.20
5. Your P2 = 8,820 × 10% = AED 882.00
6. Total = AED 1,411.20
